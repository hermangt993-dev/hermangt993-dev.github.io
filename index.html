Privacy Policy – Open World Daily

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Open World Daily is a fan-made GTA 6 news channel on Instagram (@openworld.daily), YouTube (@owdnews) and TikTok. This policy explains how the Open World Daily publishing tool ("the app") handles data.

Who runs the app

The app is run by the owner of Open World Daily, a private individual in Norway. Contact: hermangt.993@gmail.com.

Who uses the app

The app is used only by the owner, to prepare and upload videos to the owner's own Open World Daily accounts. It is not offered to other users.

What data the app uses

  • Access tokens from Instagram (Meta), YouTube (Google) and TikTok that the owner grants when connecting the accounts.
  • Basic account and video statistics for the owner's own accounts (for example views, likes, followers), used to see which videos perform well.
  • The videos, titles and descriptions the app creates.

The app does not collect data about viewers or other users, does not use cookies or tracking, and does not sell or share data with anyone.

Where data is stored

Access tokens are stored encrypted in Google Cloud Secret Manager. Statistics and videos are stored on the owner's own computer. Nothing is stored on public servers.

How long data is kept

Data is kept while the accounts are connected. When the owner disconnects an account, its token is deleted.

How to remove access

Access can be removed at any time:

  • Google/YouTube: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Instagram/Facebook: Settings → Apps and websites
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions

Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date.
